# Home Page — Bahasa Indonesia Copy Proposal

Tone guide applied throughout: warm, calm, unhurried, specialty-coffee proud; proper Bahasa Indonesia (KBBI-friendly), no slang, no word-for-word translation, no bureaucratic stiffness. Personal address uses "Anda" (polite). Source strings: `resources/views/home.blade.php`, `config/shop.php`.

Research grounding: Indonesian specialty brands (Tanamera: "Taste the Origins", "Dari Ngopi Jadi Ngerti"; Fore Coffee: "menyajikan satu cangkir kebahagiaan", "biji kopi Indonesia pilihan") consistently use "kopi spesial" as the accepted term for specialty coffee, favour short imperative CTAs ("Selengkapnya", "Unduh sekarang"), and write warm, sensory copy with verbs like "disangrai", "diseduh", "dinikmati". "single-origin", "batch", and app names (QRIS, GoFood, GrabFood, WhatsApp) are standard absorbed terms kept as-is.

---

## 1. Hero eyebrow: "Slow brew, since 2015"

| Option | Rationale |
| --- | --- |
| **Diseduh perlahan, sejak 2015** | "Diseduh perlahan" names the ritual (seduh = slow brewing verb) and mirrors the original rhythm; "perlahan" is the poetic KBBI adverb, more unhurried than "pelan". |
| Seduh pelan, sejak 2015 | Shorter, punchier, but "pelan" sits closer to spoken/banter register than the brand's calm premium mood. |
| Perlahan sejak 2015 | Too sparse — loses the brewing reference that makes the eyebrow meaningful. |

**RECOMMENDED:** Diseduh perlahan, sejak 2015

## 2. Hero headline: "Coffee worth slowing down for." (amber span wraps the emphasized phrase)

| Option | Rationale |

## 3. Hero subtext: "Single-origin beans, roasted in small batches and brewed to order. A calm corner in the middle of your day."

| Option | Rationale |
| --- | --- |
| **Biji single-origin, disangrai dalam batch kecil, diseduh segar saat pesanan masuk. Sudut yang tenang di tengah hari yang sibuk.** | "saat pesanan masuk" is a native service phrase; "di tengah hari yang sibuk" gives the "calm corner" line a purpose (the contrast is the point). |
